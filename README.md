# G-Monitor Talkback

Local Wi-Fi voice communication for your team. This repository contains Android releases of G-Monitor Talkback by **Rommel Cuneta**.

## Download and connect

1. Download the latest APK from this repository’s **Releases** section and install it.
2. Connect your devices to the same Wi-Fi or the transmitter’s hotspot.
3. Start a **Transmitter** session. Receivers can scan its QR in the app, find it on the network, or enter its six-digit pairing PIN.
4. The transmitter accepts the request. Wait for **Connected**, then toggle **Talk** and choose who hears you.

Android sessions work without internet. iPhone and iPad receivers can use the Android transmitter’s local web app after completing its **iOS setup**, including one-time certificate trust per Android installation. Keep the apps open during a session.

**Current release: v1.7.4 — Android preview.** Device compatibility is still being validated; Samsung S24 Ultra echo/feedback remains unresolved.
